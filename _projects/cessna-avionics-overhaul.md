**Senior project, B.S. Aviation Technologies (Avionics), Southern Illinois University. Team of three; all work shared.**

The full avionics suite of a Cessna 172-type aircraft — radios, GPS, display units, ELT, audio and breaker panels — was installed on a test bench as it would be in the cockpit. We stripped the existing equipment and wiring and rebuilt the installation from scratch.

- **Rewiring:** every circuit re-run and labeled from the aircraft wiring manual and the equipment manuals, following AC 43.13 practices for wire routing, termination, and grounding.
- **Verification:** continuity and wiring checks on every harness before power-up, then a powered functional test of each unit.
- **Troubleshooting:** miswired connections found during the rebuild were documented and corrected before the functional test.
