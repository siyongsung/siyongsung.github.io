**Aviation Maintenance Technology (A&P) program, Orange Coast College. Team of four.**

A complete overhaul of a six-cylinder horizontally-opposed Continental engine (O-300 series, the type fitted to early Cessna 172s), carried out to the manufacturer's overhaul manual.

- **Teardown and inspection:** full disassembly, cleaning, and visual inspection of every component, with parts tagged and tracked through the process.
- **Dimensional inspection:** every measurement called out in the manual's table of limits — cylinder bore and out-of-round, valve guides, stems and seats, valve spring load, piston rings, crankshaft and camshaft journals and runout, bearing clearances — taken with micrometers, bore and dial gauges, and a spring tester, and recorded against the serviceable limits. All measured parts were within limits.
- **Reassembly:** rebuilt with every item the manual requires to be replaced at overhaul, torqued to specification, and documented on a release certificate.
